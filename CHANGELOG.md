### 2026-09-16 — Why in-place turning cannot be trained away / 为什么"原地转"不是训练能解决的

- **The walking section of the [capability board](docs/capabilities.md) / [能力清单](docs/capabilities.zh-CN.md) now states *why* in-place turning fails, instead of listing it as an unfinished item.** A new subsection, [Why in-place turning cannot be trained away](docs/capabilities.md#why-in-place-turning-cannot-be-trained-away) / [为什么"原地转"不是训练能解决的](docs/capabilities.zh-CN.md#为什么原地转不是训练能解决的), reports three hard constraints, each with an analytic bound **and** a measured value from the same scene: the hips' yaw joints are an **internal torque pair** with a net moment of zero, so with both feet planted they can twist the torso only **±30.5°** relative to the ground (measured: **8.0°** of foot slip plus **22.5°** of torso = **30.5°**); unloading one foot would need a **59.8–60.2 mm** sideways CoM shift (stance **171.4 mm**, foot width **51.1 mm**), and the CoP was measured to equal the CoM projection to **0.000 mm**, so the requirement is exact; and with **no ankle roll** the only lever left to shift the CoM is the torso rotating about the hip-roll axes, giving a symmetric bound of **35.3 mm** (**16.5 mm** measured) or **76.0 mm** with one leg forced to its limit — measured **38.5 mm**, at which point the robot is already tilted **28.5°** against a **30°** fall line. / **能力清单的行走一节现在写明"原地转"失败的原因，而不是把它列为未完成项。** 新增小节给出三条硬约束，每条都有**解析上界**和**同一场景里的实测值**：两个髋偏航关节是**内力矩对**、对整机力矩和恒为 0，所以两脚贴地时只能让躯干相对地面扭 **±30.5°**（实测：脚打滑 **8.0°** 加躯干 **22.5°** = **30.5°**）；卸载一只脚需要质心横移 **59.8–60.2 mm**（站距 **171.4 mm**、脚宽 **51.1 mm**），而 CoP 与质心投影实测相等、差 **0.000 mm**，所以这个要求是精确的；而在**没有踝侧摆**的前提下，能挪动质心的唯一力臂是躯干绕髋侧摆轴转，对称上界 **35.3 mm**（实测 **16.5 mm**）、把一条腿掰到限位 **76.0 mm** —— 实测 **38.5 mm**，而那时机器人已倾 **28.5°**，摔倒线是 **30°**。
- **Eleven drive patterns were tried, and none of them accumulates.** Triangle waves, sine scrubbing, scissoring, a four-phase ratchet, and slow ramps into both end stops were run for 10 s each on the **unmodified training MJCF**: the seven that stayed upright reoriented the **feet in the world** by only **−12° to +8°** against a **286°** command, and every pattern that turned further was on the ground within **0.96–1.22 s** (post-fall tumbling excluded). Lifting one foot does reach **0.000 N** of load, but only at the joint limits, at **28.47°** of tilt, for **0.8 %** of the time — that is falling onto one foot, not a controlled single support. / **试了 11 种驱动方式，没有一种能累积。** 三角波、正弦搓地、剪刀式、四相棘轮、慢升到两个限位，各在**未经改动的训练用 MJCF** 上跑 10 s：站满 10 s 的 7 种，**脚在世界系里**只重定向 **−12° 到 +8°**（指令是 **286°**）；转得更多的那几种**都在 0.96–1.22 s 内倒地**（倒地后的翻滚已剔除）。抬一只脚确实能把法向力压到 **0.000 N**，但只在关节限位处、倾角 **28.47°**、时间占比 **0.8 %** 时出现 —— 那是"倒在一只脚上"，不是可控单支撑。
- **The deliverable is restated, and so is the fix.** Turning happens **while moving**. Measured with the published policy through the published runner (10 s runs, yaw command **0.5 rad/s = 28.6 deg/s**): at **0.10 and 0.15 m/s the robot does not walk at all** — **0.012–0.018 m** of travel in 10 s, with or without the yaw command — so at those speeds the question of tracking the yaw command does not arise, and a figure like "1 % yaw tracking at 0.15 m/s" describes a robot that is standing still. From **0.20 m/s** it does turn while walking: **62 % / 69 % / 75 %** of the command at 0.20 / 0.30 / 0.40 m/s, on arcs of **measured** radius **0.72 / 0.93 / 1.11 m**, which is larger than the **commanded** ratio vx / \|wz\| (0.40 / 0.60 / 0.80 m) by exactly the under-tracking factor. The tightest arc actually **measured** is therefore **R ≈ 0.7 m** at **vx ≥ 0.20 m/s**. Standing still, the torso can be re-oriented **once** by up to **±30.5°** relative to the ground, which is enough to aim the head or a camera but does not accumulate. True in-place rotation is a **hardware** change: an ankle-roll (or equivalent coronal-plane) degree of freedom so the legs can lean and the CoM can move ≥ 60 mm, or a waist yaw joint. [`README.md`](README.md) / [`README.zh-CN.md`](README.zh-CN.md) and [`docs/simulation.md`](docs/simulation.md) / [`docs/simulation.zh-CN.md`](docs/simulation.zh-CN.md) were reworded to match, so the repository no longer implies that another training round would fix it. / **交付口径与修法都改写清楚。** 转向发生在**行进中**。用公开策略走公开运行器实测（每次 10 s，偏航指令 **0.5 rad/s = 28.6 度/秒**）：在 **0.10 与 0.15 m/s 时机器人根本不会走** —— 10 s 只移动 **0.012–0.018 m**，给不给偏航指令都一样 —— 所以那两个速度上"偏航跟不跟得上"这个问题不成立，而"0.15 m/s 下偏航跟踪 1 %"这种说法描述的其实是一台站着不动的机器人。从 **0.20 m/s** 起它确实边走边转：在 0.20 / 0.30 / 0.40 m/s 下分别跟踪到指令的 **62 % / 69 % / 75 %**，走的是**实测**半径 **0.72 / 0.93 / 1.11 m** 的弧线，比**指令比** vx / \|wz\|（0.40 / 0.60 / 0.80 m）更大，差值正是欠跟踪的倍数。因此**实测到**的最紧弧线是 **R ≈ 0.7 m**（**vx ≥ 0.20 m/s**）。静止时躯干可以**一次性**相对地面重定向至多 **±30.5°**，够用来对准头部或相机，但不可累积。真正的原地转属于**硬件改动**：加踝侧摆（或等效冠状面自由度）让腿能侧倾、质心能横移 ≥ 60 mm，或加腰部偏航关节。README 与仿真指南的中英两版都已同步改写，本仓库不再暗示"再训一轮就能解决"。
- **How it was measured, and how the ruler was checked first.** Plain MuJoCo on **CPU**, training MJCF unmodified, **two independent runs producing byte-identical JSON**, and a self-test that calibrates the ruler before measuring anything: a box the size of the real sole, hinged about z, starts turning at **0.635 N·m**, i.e. an effective lever of **34.5 mm**, inside the analytic bracket of **25.5–47.7 mm**; it also asserts that the CoP equals the CoM projection. One negative result is recorded with it: an earlier idea of measuring the load split by welding the base to the world was discarded, because that weld carries **63.6 %** of the vertical load. / **怎么测的、尺子怎么先验的。** 纯 **CPU** MuJoCo、训练用 MJCF 原样不动、两次独立运行产出的 JSON **逐字节相同**；工具在测任何东西之前先自检标定尺子：与真实脚掌同尺寸的 box 只留绕 z 铰链，实测起转阈值 **0.635 N·m**，即等效力臂 **34.5 mm**，落在解析区间 **25.5–47.7 mm** 内；同时断言 CoP 与质心投影相等。一条反面结论也一并记录：早期想用"把基座焊死到世界"来测承重分配的办法被弃用，因为那个焊点会分走 **63.6 %** 的垂向载荷。
- **What is not claimed.** No hardware test was possible; the arc-turning figures come from **single CPU rollouts** (one run per speed, no multi-seed or multi-environment sweep), so **R ≈ 0.7 m** and the yaw-tracking percentages above 0.20 m/s should be read as indicative rather than precise. ### 2026-09-16 — Two reported `sitstand` problems, measured / 用户报告的 `sitstand` 两个问题，已实测

- A user playing the published demo reported two problems with the sit/stand policy, in their own words: with `sitstand` selected the duck **buzzes in place while standing**, and sitting down does not give "the head and torso vertical to the ground with the two legs braced symmetrically". Both were then measured — the report is quoted as the user gave it, and every number below is our own measurement taken afterwards. / 一位用户在实际玩公开 demo 时报告了坐／站策略的两个问题，原话是：切到 `sitstand` 后**鸭子站立时会原地抖动**，坐下也不是"头和躯干直立于地面、左右两腿对称对撑"的坐姿。两条随后都做了实测 —— 报告按用户原话引用，下面每个数字都是我们事后自己测的。
- The measurement used this repository's own published single-file runner, **CPU only**, MuJoCo **3.10.0** / ONNX Runtime **1.30.0** / NumPy **2.5.3**, 500 control steps (**10.0 s**) per run, spawn `nominal`, flat floor — `python wamoduck_sim.py --policy sitstand --target-height 0.175 --headless --steps 500`, the same command at `0.085`, and `python wamoduck_sim.py --policy stand --headless --steps 500` as the control. The per-step traces were recorded with a temporary harness outside the repository that drives `wamoduck_sim.Demo` and the same observe → act → apply → step loop the headless mode uses, so the run is the documented one and not a second implementation. / 测量用的是本仓库公开的单文件运行器，**纯 CPU**，MuJoCo **3.10.0**／ONNX Runtime **1.30.0**／NumPy **2.5.3**，每次运行 500 个控制步（**10.0 s**），出生点 `nominal`，平地 —— 命令为 `python wamoduck_sim.py --policy sitstand --target-height 0.175 --headless --steps 500`、把高度换成 `0.085` 的同一条命令、以及作为对照的 `python wamoduck_sim.py --policy stand --headless --steps 500`。逐控制步的轨迹由仓库外的一个临时测量脚本记录，它驱动的是 `wamoduck_sim.Demo` 与无界面模式同一条 observe → act → apply → step 循环，所以这是文档里写的那一次运行，不是第二套实现。
- **The two figures already on record reproduce.** The run holds **0.094 m** against a 0.085 m command and leans **~26°**, matching the numbers this repository already published for that command. What is new is the jitter and the left/right symmetry. / **已有记录的两个数字被重现了。** 这次运行在指令 0.085 m 时维持 **0.094 m**、倾斜约 **26°**，与本仓库此前公布的该指令数字一致。新测到的是**抖动**与**左右对称性**。
- **Problem 1 — the standing posture buzzes in place.** At a 0.175 m command the robot is upright (final tilt **0.44°**, base height **0.1762 m**) and never settles: action jitter, `mean abs(delta a)` per control step — the same metric row 1 of the capability board uses — is **0.229** over the whole 10 s, **0.235** after the first 1.0 s and **0.236** in the last 1.0 s, with a maximum single step of **1.313**; tilt std **0.403°** (**0.396°** in the last second, so it is a sustained limit cycle rather than a settling transient); base-height std **1.10 mm**; mean absolute joint velocity **1.69 rad/s** with a peak of **10.11 rad/s**. The `stand` policy on the same metric and the same run length gives **9.9e-5**, **2.9e-7** and **4.1e-8**, i.e. about **six orders of magnitude** quieter, so this is a property of the sit/stand policy and not of the runner. Both soles are down in only **92.2 %** of the steps after the first second and the feet carry **45.46 N** on average against a **36.80 N** weight — the buzz is pushing the ground — and "in place" is approximate, because it also travels **0.263 m** over the 10 s. / **问题一 —— 站立姿态原地抖动。** 0.175 m 指令下机器人是直立的（末态倾角 **0.44°**、基座高度 **0.1762 m**），但**始终不收敛**：动作抖动（每控制步的 `mean abs(delta a)`，正是能力清单第 1 行所用的那个指标）整 10 s 为 **0.229**、去掉前 1.0 s 为 **0.235**、末 1.0 s 为 **0.236**，单步最大 **1.313**；倾角标准差 **0.403°**（末 1 s 为 **0.396°**，所以这是持续的极限环而不是会衰减的过渡过程）；基座高度标准差 **1.10 mm**；关节速度绝对值均值 **1.69 rad/s**、峰值 **10.11 rad/s**。同一个指标、同样运行长度下 `stand` 策略是 **9.9e-5**、**2.9e-7**、**4.1e-8**，安静约**六个数量级**，所以这是坐／站策略本身的性质、不是运行器的性质。去掉前 1 s 后只有 **92.2 %** 的步数双脚同时着地，双脚平均承担 **45.46 N** 而整机重量是 **36.80 N** —— 抖动的力是蹬在地面上的 —— 而"原地"也只是近似：10 s 里它同时还挪了 **0.263 m**。
- **Problem 2 — the sitting posture is neither upright nor left/right symmetric.** At a 0.085 m command the pose is **quiet** (it reaches a static pose inside about 1 s: `mean abs(delta a)` **4.4e-7** and tilt std **1.3e-4°** in the last second), so the jitter is a standing-height problem only; pressing `m` mid-run lands on the same pose. But that quiet pose is a **25.89°** lean (roll **−15.99°**, pitch **+20.64°**) held at **0.0943 m** instead of the commanded 0.085 m (**+9.3 mm**), with the head — `head_roll_link` — **25.62°** off vertical and the neck **25.44°** off, because the head chain's own joints are all within **0.6°** of zero and simply inherit the trunk's lean. It is also not standing on two feet: `base_link_body_collision`, the pelvis, is in contact with the floor in **100 %** of the last second carrying **7.16 N** of the **36.83 N** total (**19.4 %** of the weight), and the right foot rests on a single edge contact **22 mm** above its sole geometry while the left foot is flat on three. / **问题二 —— 坐姿既不直立也不左右对称。** 0.085 m 指令下这个姿态是**安静的**（约 1 s 内到达静态姿态：末 1 s 的 `mean abs(delta a)` 为 **4.4e-7**、倾角标准差 **1.3e-4°**），所以抖动只是站立高度的问题；运行途中按 `m` 切过去也落到同一个姿态。但那个安静的姿态是 **25.89°** 的倾斜（横滚 **−15.99°**、俯仰 **+20.64°**），维持在 **0.0943 m** 而不是指令的 0.085 m（**+9.3 mm**）；**头**（`head_roll_link`）偏离竖直 **25.62°**、颈 **25.44°** —— 因为头链自身的关节全部在零附近 **0.6°** 以内，只是整体继承了躯干的倾斜。它也不是靠两只脚站着：`base_link_body_collision`（骨盆）在末 1 s 的 **100 %** 步数里与地面接触，承担 **36.83 N** 里的 **7.16 N**（体重的 **19.4 %**）；右脚靠单点边缘接触立着、比脚底几何高 **22 mm**，而左脚是三点平贴。
- **The left/right symmetry was measured with the sign convention stated first, because the two sides do not share a sign.** The MJCF mirrors the ranges of the two `hip_yaw` and two `hip_roll` joints (`left_hip_yaw` **−1.7279 to +0.5323 rad** against `right_hip_yaw` **−0.5323 to +1.7279 rad**; `left_hip_roll` **−1.1153 to +0.4311 rad** against `right_hip_roll` **−0.4311 to +1.1153 rad**), while `hip_pitch`, `knee` and `ankle` have identical ranges on both sides; a direct probe agrees — `left_hip_roll = +0.3 rad` moves the left sole outward from y = **+0.0846 m** to **+0.1116 m** while `right_hip_roll = +0.3 rad` moves the right sole inward from y = **−0.0846 m** to **−0.0523 m**.
